Service Overview

Dishwasher Repair: What We Do

A dishwasher that leaves dishes dirty or water in the tub is frustrating, and the cause is usually a pump, valve, spray arm or latch. We diagnose and fix it.

Modern dishwashers rely on sensors, pumps and control boards in addition to heaters and spray arms. We test each part to find why the cycle fails.

From leaks to error codes, we explain what failed and how to keep the machine running well.

What a visit covers

  • Drain pump and filter inspection
  • Spray arm and wash pump check
  • Inlet valve and heater testing
  • Door latch and seal inspection
  • Control board and sensor diagnostics
  • Leak path inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Dishwasher repair

  • Not draining
  • Dishes not clean
  • Leaking from the door or base
  • Not starting
  • Door will not latch
  • Not filling
  • Not drying
  • Loud noise
  • Error code
  • Detergent not dispensing
  • Foul odor
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Clogged drain or pump

Food debris blocks the filter or pump.

Failed inlet valve

The dishwasher does not fill properly.

Worn door seal or latch

Leaks and no-start problems follow.

Heating element failure

Water is not hot enough to clean or dry.

Spray arm problems

Blocked or damaged arms reduce cleaning.

Control board faults

Electronic issues can stop cycles.
