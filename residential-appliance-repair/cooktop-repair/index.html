Service Overview

Cooktop Repair: What We Do

A cooktop with a dead burner or flickering display turns every meal into a workaround. We diagnose igniters, switches, elements and control boards.

Gas, radiant and induction cooktops are very different, so the first step is identifying the type and testing the parts that matter for that style.

From cracked glass to failed controls, we explain what happened and what repair makes sense.

What a visit covers

  • Burner and igniter check
  • Switch and element testing
  • Induction coil and generator diagnostics
  • Touch control and display diagnostics
  • Glass top inspection
  • Safety check
Symptoms

Signs You Need Cooktop repair

  • Burner will not light
  • Element not heating
  • Induction not detecting pans
  • Display blank or flashing
  • Burner stays on high
  • Clicking noise
  • Touch controls unresponsive
  • Cracked glass
  • Burner too weak
  • Burner turns off
  • Error code
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Dirty or failed igniter

Gas burners will not light.

Bad infinite switch

Electric burners fail to heat or control properly.

Induction board failures

Induction cooktops can lose power or detection.

Control panel faults

Touch controls can fail from heat or moisture.

Failed element

Radiant elements wear out.

Glass damage

Cracks can affect safety and operation.
