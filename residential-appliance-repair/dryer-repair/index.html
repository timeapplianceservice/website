Service Overview

Dryer Repair: What We Do

A dryer that takes two cycles is wasting energy and wearing your clothes. We diagnose heating, airflow and drive problems and repair the cause.

Gas and electric dryers fail in different ways, but airflow problems are involved in many calls. We check the vent along with the dryer to keep operation safe.

From a burnt-out heating element to a worn belt or roller, we find the failure and fix it, then explain how to keep the dryer running well.

What a visit covers

  • Heating element or gas igniter test
  • Thermal fuse and thermostat testing
  • Drum belt, rollers and idler pulley check
  • Motor and blower inspection
  • Vent and lint path check
  • Control and door switch diagnostics
Symptoms

Signs You Need Dryer repair

  • Dryer not heating
  • Dryer not tumbling
  • Clothes take too long to dry
  • Dryer will not start
  • Dryer overheating
  • Loud squealing or thumping
  • Burning smell
  • Door switch problems
  • Lint buildup
  • Timer or control not working
  • Dryer stops mid-cycle
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Failed heating element or igniter

The dryer runs but does not heat.

Tripped thermal fuse

Restricted airflow can blow the safety fuse.

Broken belt or worn rollers

The drum stops tumbling or makes noise.

Clogged vent

Poor airflow causes long drying times and overheating.

Failed motor or blower

The dryer will not run or has weak airflow.

Control or switch faults

Door and start switches may fail.
