Service Overview

Freezer Repair: What We Do

A freezer that warms up can ruin stored food in hours. We diagnose cooling, defrost and airflow problems and repair the cause so the freezer holds temperature again.

Home freezers rely on a sealed system, fans and a defrost cycle that are easy to disturb. We test each in turn and explain our findings plainly.

Whether it is a garage chest freezer or a built-in column, we diagnose the problem, quote the repair and fix it with attention to parts availability.

What a visit covers

  • Temperature verification
  • Defrost heater, termination and timer testing
  • Evaporator and condenser fan inspection
  • Door gasket check
  • Compressor and start component testing
  • Drain and frost pattern inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Freezer repair

  • Freezer not freezing
  • Food thawing or soft
  • Heavy frost build-up
  • Ice on the back wall
  • Compressor running nonstop
  • Freezer making noise
  • Water pooling under the unit
  • Door not sealing
  • Light or control not working
  • Frost on the door gasket
  • Warm freezer after power outage
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Defrost failure

A failed heater, sensor or timer lets frost accumulate.

Evaporator fan not running

Without airflow the freezer warms.

Gasket leaks

Warm air entering the freezer creates frost.

Dirty condenser

Poor heat release overworks the compressor.

Compressor start parts

Relays and capacitors can fail.

Control faults

Thermostats and boards may misread temperatures.
