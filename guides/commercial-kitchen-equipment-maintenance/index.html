A kitchen runs on its equipment. Preventive maintenance keeps ovens heating evenly, fryers holding temperature and dishmachines sanitizing properly, and it prevents the breakdowns that always seem to happen on the busiest night. Here is a practical approach.

In this guide

  • Daily habits that prevent most failures
  • Ovens and ranges
  • Fryers and griddles
  • Steamers, dishmachines and coffee equipment
  • Annual professional service

Daily habits that prevent most failures

  • Clean up spills and grease before they bake onto burners, elements and controls.
  • Empty and clean fryer filters and scrap screens in dishmachines on the schedule your equipment requires.
  • Check pilot flames and burner flames for a steady, even color.
  • Wipe door gaskets so they seal properly.
  • Report unusual smells, noises or slow heat-up so a problem is caught early.

Ovens and ranges

Keep burner ports clear and check that igniters spark cleanly. Test the oven temperature with a thermometer a few times a year and recalibrate if it drifts. Inspect door gaskets, hinges and latches, and listen for convection fan noise that may signal worn bearings.

Fryers and griddles

Keep fryer oil clean, check the filtration system and verify the thermostat and high-limit operation. On griddles, look for cold spots that signal clogged burners or a failing thermostat, and keep grease troughs clear.

Steamers, dishmachines and coffee equipment

Hard water causes scale in steamers, dishmachines and coffee brewers, so filtration and regular descaling are important. Check dishmachine wash and rinse temperatures and chemical feed on a regular basis, and clean spray arms and jets. Descale coffee brewers on a schedule and check spray heads and fittings for leaks.

Annual professional service

Schedule a professional inspection of your key equipment once or twice a year. We test safety devices, check gas and electrical components, calibrate thermostats and replace worn gaskets and parts before they fail. A service visit before your busy season is especially valuable.

If you smell gas at any time, leave the area and call your gas utility first.
