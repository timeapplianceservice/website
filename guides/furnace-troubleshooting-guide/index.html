A furnace that will not heat on a cold night is more than an annoyance. Some causes are simple to check, and others need a technician. This guide shows what you can safely check and when to call.

In this guide

  • Safety first
  • Check the thermostat and settings
  • Check power and the furnace switch
  • Check the filter and airflow
  • What the sounds and lights can tell you
  • When to call a technician

Safety first

If you smell gas, leave the home immediately, avoid switches, flames and phones inside, and call your gas utility or emergency services from outside. Do not try to operate the furnace. If you smell burning or see smoke, turn the system off and call for help.

Check the thermostat and settings

Set the thermostat to heat and raise the temperature several degrees above the current reading. Check the batteries if the display is dim or blank. Make sure the fan setting is auto and any schedule or hold is not overriding your setting.

Check power and the furnace switch

Look at the breaker for the furnace and the wall switch near the unit, which sometimes gets turned off by accident. Make sure the blower compartment panel is seated properly, because a safety switch stops the furnace if the door is not closed.

Check the filter and airflow

A clogged filter restricts airflow and can overheat the furnace, causing it to shut off on a safety limit. Change a dirty filter and open all vents. Furnaces that start, run a few minutes and shut off are often short cycling because of airflow or a dirty flame sensor.

What the sounds and lights can tell you

Many furnaces have a small light on the control board that blinks a code. Note the pattern, because it points to the fault, such as pressure switch, ignition or limit problems. Clicking without ignition suggests an igniter or gas valve issue. Squealing or grinding points to the blower or inducer motor. Banging at startup can be delayed ignition and should be checked promptly.

When to call a technician

We diagnose and repair existing furnaces, and our service call is free when you approve the repair.

  • The burner lights then shuts off repeatedly.
  • The furnace cycles on and off rapidly.
  • There is no heat after you have checked the thermostat, power and filter.
  • You hear loud or unusual noises.
  • The control board shows an error code.
