A little preparation helps your technician diagnose faster and often fix the appliance in a single visit. Here is what to do before we arrive and what to expect during the visit.

In this guide

  • Gather basic information
  • Clear access
  • Keep safety in mind
  • What happens during the visit
  • After the repair

Gather basic information

  • The brand, model number and serial number, usually on a label inside the door or on the back.
  • What the appliance is doing, when it started and anything that changed, such as a power outage or a moved appliance.
  • Any error codes displayed.
  • Whether you have tried a reset or any fix already.

Clear access

Make sure the technician can reach the appliance. Empty the refrigerator section if needed, clear the area around washers and dryers and pull out items under or beside built-in units. Secure pets and let us know if there are access considerations such as stairs, gates or parking.

Keep safety in mind

If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first. If water is leaking, shut off the water supply to the appliance if you can. For a refrigerator, keep the doors closed to hold the cold, and move perishables to a cooler if the problem will take time.

What happens during the visit

The technician confirms the symptoms, tests the appliance and explains the cause. You will receive a clear repair quote before any work begins. If you approve the repair, the $135 service call is free. If you decide not to repair, the service call applies. Our $50 off labor offer is described on our pages.

When parts are on the truck, many repairs are completed during the visit. If a part must be ordered, we explain the timeline.

After the repair

Your repair includes a 30-day labor warranty and a 90-day parts warranty. Keep your invoice, and call us if the same problem returns. We also share simple tips to keep the appliance running well, such as cleaning condenser coils or lint filters.
