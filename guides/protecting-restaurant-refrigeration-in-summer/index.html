Summer is when marginal refrigeration fails. Heat raises condensing pressure, kitchens get hotter and doors open more often. A few preventive steps before the heat arrives can save you from losing product during your busiest season.

In this guide

  • Why summer is hard on refrigeration
  • Clean condensers before the heat
  • Check fans, gaskets and doors
  • Protect ice machines
  • Plan for emergencies

Why summer is hard on refrigeration

Condensers reject heat into the surrounding air, and when that air is hot the system works much harder. Dirty condensers, weak fans and failing electrical parts that were fine in spring suddenly cannot keep up. Humidity adds to the load by increasing frost and condensate.

Kitchens also run hotter in summer, which raises temperatures around reach-ins, prep tables and undercounter units.

Clean condensers before the heat

A clean condenser is the single most effective step. Schedule coil cleaning on walk-in condensing units, reach-ins, prep tables and ice machines before summer. Make sure there is clearance around equipment so air can move.

Check fans, gaskets and doors

Test condenser and evaporator fans, replace worn door gaskets and check heater wires and strip curtains. Summer humidity and heat make leaking doors costly, because every opening lets in moisture that frosts the coil.

Protect ice machines

Ice machines slow down in hot rooms. Keep the area ventilated, clean the condenser and keep water filters current. If production drops in summer, do not wait for a failure to schedule a check.

Plan for emergencies

  • Know where your equipment model and serial numbers are written.
  • Keep a probe thermometer handy and log temperatures.
  • Have a plan for moving product if a unit fails.
  • Save our number. Emergency visits are available on request.
  • Schedule maintenance before peak season so we know your equipment before a crisis.
