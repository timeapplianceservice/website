What Causes This — And How We Fix It

Water around your indoor unit is almost always a condensate drainage problem — not something that requires a new system. We clear the blockage, fix the cause, and protect your home from further water damage.

What We Check

✓ Clogged condensate drain line
✓ Cracked or full condensate pan
✓ Frozen evaporator coil (thawing and dripping)
✓ Improperly sloped drain line
✓ Failed condensate pump

What to Have Ready When You Call

  • • Where exactly the water is coming from
  • • How long it's been leaking
  • • Whether the system is still cooling
