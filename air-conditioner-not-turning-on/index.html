What Causes This — And How We Fix It

An AC that won't power on at all is often an electrical or control issue rather than a failed compressor. We check the most common causes first so you're not paying for a bigger repair than you need.

What We Check

✓ Tripped breaker or blown fuse
✓ Failed capacitor or contactor
✓ Thermostat wiring or battery issue
✓ Tripped float switch (condensate safety)
✓ Faulty disconnect switch at the outdoor unit

What to Have Ready When You Call

  • • Whether the thermostat display is on
  • • Whether you've checked the breaker panel
  • • Whether the outdoor unit hums or is silent
  • • Any error codes on a smart thermostat
