What Causes This — And How We Fix It

The run/start capacitor is one of the most common points of failure in a residential AC system — it's a quick, affordable repair on your existing unit, not a reason to replace anything else.

What We Check

✓ Humming sound with no fan movement
✓ AC struggling to start (hard start)
✓ Outdoor fan not spinning
✓ Capacitor case bulging or leaking

What to Have Ready When You Call

  • • Whether you hear humming from the outdoor unit
  • • Whether the fan blades spin freely by hand (power off first)
  • • System brand and approximate age
