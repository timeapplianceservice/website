Service Overview

Commercial Refrigeration Maintenance: What We Do

Most refrigeration emergencies start as small, fixable problems. Regular maintenance keeps coils clean, parts tested and temperatures stable, so surprises are rare.

A planned maintenance visit is far cheaper than a failure on a busy Saturday. We clean, test and document your refrigeration equipment on a schedule that fits your operation.

Maintenance also supports food safety and efficiency. Clean coils, healthy fans and sound gaskets mean steadier temperatures and lower energy use.

What a visit covers

  • Condenser and evaporator coil cleaning
  • Fan motor, bearing and blade inspection
  • Door gasket, heater and hinge checks
  • Electrical connections and component testing
  • Drain and defrost system checks
  • Temperature log review and written report
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial refrigeration maintenance

  • Equipment running longer than before
  • Rising energy bills
  • Frost or ice appearing more often
  • Temperatures drifting between visits
  • Noises that were not there before
  • Gaskets showing wear
  • Preparing for summer peak season
  • Health inspection coming up
  • No maintenance since installation
  • Multiple units of different ages
  • Desire to avoid emergency calls

Defrost and drain checks
