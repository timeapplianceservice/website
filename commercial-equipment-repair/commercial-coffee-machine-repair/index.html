Service Overview

Commercial Coffee Machine Repair: What We Do

Coffee is the first order of the day, so a brewer that fails at opening is a real problem. We diagnose heating, water flow, valves and controls on commercial brewers and urns.

Commercial coffee equipment depends on clean water and working heating systems. We repair the parts that fail: elements, valves, sensors, pumps and control boards.

Whether you run a bakery, an office kitchen or a busy cafe, we service brewers, urns and espresso machines with attention to your service hours.

What a visit covers

  • Water temperature and flow verification
  • Heating element and thermostat testing
  • Inlet valve and solenoid inspection
  • Spray head and tube cleaning
  • Leak and fitting inspection
  • Control board and sensor checks
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial coffee machine repair

  • Coffee not hot
  • Brewer not heating water
  • Water not flowing
  • Brewer leaking
  • Weak or slow brewing
  • Machine not starting
  • Error code on the display
  • Spray head clogged
  • Steam wand not working
  • Urn dripping
  • Burnt or off taste
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Scale buildup

Mineral deposits restrict water flow and coat heating elements.

Heating element or thermostat failure

The machine cannot reach brewing temperature.

Inlet valve problems

Failed valves reduce or stop water flow.

Clogged spray heads or tubing

Debris blocks water distribution.

Leaking fittings and gaskets

Wear causes drips and low water pressure.

Control faults

Electronic controls can fail and prevent normal operation.
