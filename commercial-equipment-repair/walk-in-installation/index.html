Service Overview

Walk-In Cooler & Freezer Installation: What We Do

A new walk-in is a major investment, and the installation determines how well it performs for years. We plan the layout, assemble the box, set up the refrigeration system and verify temperatures at startup.

Whether you are adding a walk-in to a new restaurant or expanding an existing kitchen, proper sizing, door placement and refrigeration setup matter. We walk through the options with you before work begins.

From panel assembly to the condensing unit and controls, we handle the installation steps and verify performance before turning the box over to you.

What a visit covers

  • Site visit and layout planning
  • Box assembly and door placement
  • Evaporator and condensing unit setup
  • Electrical and control connections
  • Startup, temperature pull-down and verification
  • Operating guidance and documentation

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Right-sizing the box

Capacity, product load and door traffic determine the size and refrigeration load we plan for.

Location and airflow

Placement affects airflow, condensing unit heat rejection and access for service.

Electrical requirements

Power supply, disconnects and controls must match the equipment.

Floor, drain and utilities

Drains, floors and clearances are planned so the box works as designed.

Startup and verification

We verify pull-down time and temperature stability before handoff.

Permits and inspections

Local requirements are reviewed so installation follows jurisdiction rules.
