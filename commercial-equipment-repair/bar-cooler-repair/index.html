Service Overview

Bar Cooler Repair: What We Do

A back bar cooler that cannot keep up costs sales on the busiest nights. We service bottle coolers, back bar refrigerators and glass-door units to keep drinks cold.

Bar coolers get opened constantly and sit in warm, crowded spaces. We check airflow, condensers, gaskets and fans, and repair the part that is causing the problem.

From neighborhood pubs to brewery taprooms, we service the refrigeration behind the bar with technicians who understand the pace of service.

What a visit covers

  • Interior temperature and door gasket check
  • Condenser coil and fan testing
  • Evaporator fan and defrost review
  • Glass door and hinge inspection
  • Light and control checks
  • Drain and condensate check
Symptoms

Signs You Need Bar cooler repair

  • Drinks not cold enough
  • Cooler running nonstop
  • Glass doors fogging or frosting
  • Fans loud or stopped
  • Gaskets torn or loose
  • Water leaking under the unit
  • Cooler warm after restocking
  • Lights out or flickering
  • Compressor clicking
  • Condenser coil caked with dust
  • Temperature swings during service
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Heat and airflow

Tight installations and hot bar environments push condensers beyond their limits.

Gasket and door wear

Frequent opening wears gaskets and hinges quickly, which lets cold air out.

Fan motor failure

A failed fan limits airflow and temperature.

Electrical wear

Start parts and relays weaken from repeated cycling.

Dirty condenser

Dust and bar debris reduce cooling capacity.

Defrost or sensor faults

Ice and wrong readings follow control problems.
