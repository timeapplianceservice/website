Service Overview

What to Expect From Walk-In Freezer Repair

A walk-in freezer works harder than a cooler, and its defrost system is the part most likely to fall behind. We test defrost heaters, timers and terminations along with the compressor and fans.

What a visit covers

  • Condensing unit and compressor electrical checks
  • Clear written repair options
  • Box and product temperature verification
  • Evaporator coil, fans and drain heaters
  • Defrost heater, termination and timer testing
  • Door heater, gasket and strip curtain inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Walk-in freezer repair in Monroe

  • Frost on door gaskets
  • Controller alarm for high temperature
  • Drain line frozen or clogged
  • Thick ice on evaporator or fan guard
  • Product with freezer burn or ice crystals
  • Defrost never finishing
  • Fans inside box not running
  • Ice buildup on the floor or ceiling
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Evaporator fan problems

A stalled or ice-bound fan stops circulating air, and the box temperature rises quickly.

Compressor electrical issues

Start capacitors and contactors that wear out keep the compressor from running, even when it looks fine.

Refrigerant circuit issues

A leak or restriction reduces capacity, so the freezer runs constantly and still cannot hold temperature.

Frozen drain line

A drain line without a working heater freezes shut, sending meltwater back as ice onto the floor and coil.
