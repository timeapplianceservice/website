Service Overview

What to Expect From Evaporator Coil and Fan Repair

Evaporator fans and coils sit in cold, wet conditions, so motors, heaters and drains wear faster than other parts. We service all of them.

What a visit covers

  • Door infiltration assessment
  • Fan guard and shroud inspection
  • Defrost heater, timer and termination test
  • Drain pan, line and heater check
  • Evaporator fan motor and blade test
  • Coil condition and ice pattern inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Evaporator coil and fan repair in Davidson

  • Fans not turning or noisy
  • Frozen drain pan
  • Water dripping from the evaporator
  • Refrigerant lines sweating heavily
  • Short defrost cycles
  • Ice covering the coil
  • Unit alarming for high temperature
  • Frost pattern on one side only
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Refrigerant issues

Low charge or restrictions create uneven frost patterns.

Low airflow from product loading

Crowded shelving blocks air and promotes icing.

Fan motor failure

Worn bearings or failed motors stop airflow.

Failed defrost components

Heaters, timers and sensors that stop working let ice accumulate.
