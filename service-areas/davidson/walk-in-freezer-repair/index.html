Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Defrost heater or termination failure

If the heater, timer or termination sensor fails, ice builds until it blocks the coil and the freezer warms.

Compressor electrical issues

Start capacitors and contactors that wear out keep the compressor from running, even when it looks fine.

Evaporator fan problems

A stalled or ice-bound fan stops circulating air, and the box temperature rises quickly.

Frozen drain line

A drain line without a working heater freezes shut, sending meltwater back as ice onto the floor and coil.
