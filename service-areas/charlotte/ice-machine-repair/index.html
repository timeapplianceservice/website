Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Refrigeration faults

Compressor or refrigerant circuit issues reduce capacity.

Water supply issues

A clogged filter or failing inlet valve starves the machine of water.

Harvest and sensor faults

Bad thermistors, probes or bin controls prevent proper harvest or shut-off.

Scale and water quality

Mineral buildup coats the evaporator and sensors and reduces ice production.
