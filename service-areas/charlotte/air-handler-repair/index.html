Service Overview

What to Expect From Air Handler Repair

The air handler is the indoor half of many heat pump and AC systems, and it houses the blower, controls and often electric heat. We test each part to find the fault.

What a visit covers

  • Electric heat element and sequencer testing
  • Control board and relay testing
  • Filter and coil condition inspection
  • Safety switch and sensor checks
  • Blower motor and capacitor check
  • Wiring and connection inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Air handler repair in Charlotte

  • Burning smell
  • Breaker tripping
  • Blower running constantly
  • Humming or clicking
  • Heat strips not heating
  • Error code on the board
  • Thermostat connection problems
  • Air handler not turning on
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Safety switch trips

Float or limit switches shut the unit down.

Restricted airflow

Clogged filters and coils strain components.

Electric heat failures

Elements, limits and sequencers can fail.

Relay and wiring faults

Loose or burnt connections interrupt operation.
