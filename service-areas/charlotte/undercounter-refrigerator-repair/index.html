Service Overview

What to Expect From Undercounter Refrigerator Repair

Undercounter units work in cramped, hot spaces with limited airflow, so condenser problems show up early. We clean, test and repair these compact systems quickly.

What a visit covers

  • Door hinge and closer adjustment
  • Condenser coil access and cleaning check
  • Fan motor and control testing
  • Drain and water management check
  • Compressor start components test
  • Interior temperature and gasket inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Undercounter refrigerator repair in Charlotte

  • Door not sealing
  • Cabinet icing on the back wall
  • Unit warm after heavy use
  • Compressor running constantly
  • Cabinet not cooling
  • Water under the cabinet
  • Fan noise or no airflow
  • Unit cycles on and off rapidly
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Clogged drain

Blocked condensate lines drip onto the floor or back up into the cabinet.

Control or sensor failure

A faulty thermostat or sensor can cause freezing or warm temperatures.

Failed fan motors

A stopped fan leaves warm spots or lets the coil ice over.

Electrical start parts

Weak capacitors and relays can stop the compressor from starting.
