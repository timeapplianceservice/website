Service Overview

What to Expect From Blower Motor Repair

The blower moves air through your whole house, so when it fails the system cannot heat or cool. We test the motor, capacitor and control to find what is limiting airflow.

What a visit covers

  • Control module and wiring check
  • Filter and coil restriction check
  • Motor amp draw and bearing check
  • Capacitor testing
  • Blower wheel inspection
  • Airflow measurements
Symptoms

Signs You Need Blower motor repair in Charlotte

  • Intermittent blower operation
  • Blower noisy or squealing
  • Clicking or humming from the air handler
  • Blower running constantly
  • Blower not running
  • Furnace shutting off on limit
  • AC freezing from low airflow
  • Burning smell from the air handler
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Control module faults

Variable-speed motors depend on a working control module.

Worn bearings

Aging motors develop noise and eventually seize.

Failed capacitor

A weak capacitor prevents the motor from starting or running at speed.

Dirty blower wheel

Dust on the wheel reduces airflow and throws it off balance.
