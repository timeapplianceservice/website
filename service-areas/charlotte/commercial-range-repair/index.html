  • Weak or uneven flame

  • Oven door not sealing

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Electric element or switch failure

Elements and infinite switches wear out from heat and cycling.

Ignition electrode faults

Damaged or dirty electrodes prevent reliable lighting.

Gas valve problems

A sticking or failing valve causes burners not to light or to run wrong.

Oven thermostat failure

A bad thermostat misreads temperature and leads to under or overheating.
