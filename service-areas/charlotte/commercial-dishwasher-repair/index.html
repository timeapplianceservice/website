Service Overview

What to Expect From Commercial Dishwasher Repair

High-temp and low-temp commercial dishwashers each have different parts and checks. We test the machine's water temperature, wash action and chemical delivery.

What a visit covers

  • Door switch and control checks
  • Heating element and booster check
  • Chemical feed and sanitizer verification
  • Wash and rinse temperature verification
  • Wash pump and arm inspection
  • Drain and fill solenoid testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial dishwasher repair in Charlotte

  • Leaking at the door or base
  • Machine not starting
  • Not filling with water
  • Excess foam or suds
  • Not reaching proper temperature
  • Dishes coming out dirty or spotty
  • Strange noises from the pump
  • Error code
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Pump or seal wear

Leaks and noise often come from worn seals and pumps.

Chemical feed issues

Tubing, pumps and injectors can fail or clog.

Clogged wash arms or jets

Debris and scale reduce spray action.

Control or door switch faults

Safety switches and boards can prevent the machine from running.
