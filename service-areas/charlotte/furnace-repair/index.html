Service Overview

What to Expect From Furnace Repair

Furnaces rely on a chain of parts, including the thermostat, inducer, igniter, flame sensor and blower. We follow that chain until we find the break and repair it.

What a visit covers

  • Inducer and blower motor testing
  • Filter, airflow and limit switch check
  • Safety control verification
  • Igniter, flame sensor and gas valve inspection (gas furnaces)
  • Heating element and sequencer testing (electric furnaces)
  • Thermostat and sequence-of-operation test
Symptoms

Signs You Need Furnace repair in Charlotte

  • Weak airflow
  • Gas smell (leave the home and call your gas utility first)
  • Clicking without ignition
  • Furnace not turning on
  • Uneven heating
  • Noisy blower or inducer
  • Furnace short cycling
  • Burner lights and goes out
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Inducer or pressure switch faults

Safety circuits prevent ignition when they do not prove draft.

Dirty flame sensor

A coated sensor cannot prove flame, so the furnace shuts the burner off after a few seconds.

Blower motor or capacitor problems

A weak motor or capacitor reduces airflow and can trigger limit switches.

Weak or cracked igniter

Igniters wear out and fail to light the burner.
