Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Water flow issues

A clogged filter or inlet valve limits fill.

Poor ventilation

Undercounter ice makers overheat when airflow is blocked.

Pump or distribution problems

Weak pumps and clogged tubes produce poor ice.

Scale buildup

Mineral scale on the evaporator and sensors lowers output and ice quality.
