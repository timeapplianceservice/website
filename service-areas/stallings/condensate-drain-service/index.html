Service Overview

What to Expect From Condensate Drain Service

Air conditioners remove gallons of moisture on humid days, and the drain line carries it away. When the line clogs, the system can shut down or leak into your home.

What a visit covers

  • Preventive recommendations
  • Float switch testing
  • Drain pan inspection
  • Drain line clearing and flush
  • Indoor coil moisture check
  • Drain line condition check
Symptoms

Signs You Need AC drain line service in Stallings

  • Slow or no drain flow
  • Musty smell near the air handler
  • Overflowing drain pan
  • Water around the indoor unit
  • Humid air despite AC running
  • Algae in the drain line
  • Condensate pump not working
  • Ice on the coil causing excess drip
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Algae and debris

Biological growth and dirt narrow the line until it clogs.

Condensate pump failure

Pumps can fail and stop moving water.

Poor drain slope

Lines that do not slope properly hold water.

Float switch trips

The safety switch stops the system when water backs up.
