Service Overview

What to Expect From Commercial Griddle Repair

Commercial griddles take constant heat and grease. We check burners, thermostats and safety devices, and repair the part causing uneven heat.

What a visit covers

  • Safety valve and control checks
  • Burner and pilot inspection on gas models
  • Heat distribution and plate temperature check
  • Thermostat and knob testing
  • Grease trough and drain inspection
  • Electric element and contactor testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial griddle repair in Stallings

  • Burner not lighting
  • Gas odor (leave the area and call your gas utility first)
  • Pilot going out
  • Griddle not heating
  • Excessive smoke
  • Thermostat clicking constantly
  • Cold spots on the griddle plate
  • Temperature not matching the dial
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Clogged or dirty burners

Grease and debris disrupt flame distribution.

Gas valve problems

Stuck valves create low or uneven heat.

Electric element failure

Elements fail and leave cold zones.

Thermostat failure

A bad thermostat misreads plate temperature.
