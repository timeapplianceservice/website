Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Weak start components

Capacitors and relays that fail prevent the compressor from starting, even though the unit seems to have power.

Door gasket failure

A loose or torn gasket leaks cold air, frosts the coil and keeps the compressor running.

Defrost problems

A failed defrost component lets ice build until the box can no longer cool.

Evaporator fan failure

A seized fan stops air movement, causing warm spots and eventually a frozen coil.
