Service Overview

What to Expect From Prep Table Repair

Sandwich, salad and pizza prep tables take heat, spills and constant door openings. We service the sealed system, fans, lids and gaskets that keep them performing.

What a visit covers

  • Lid, gasket and hinge inspection
  • Sealed system pressure and performance review
  • Rail and cabinet temperature check
  • Drain pan and tube check
  • Thermostat and sensor verification
  • Condenser and evaporator fan testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Prep table repair in Stallings

  • Compressor running constantly
  • Unit cycling rapidly
  • Controller error or blank display
  • Pans warm at the top
  • Lid not closing tightly
  • Food spoiling faster than usual
  • Water dripping inside
  • Cabinet cold but rail warm
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Dirty condenser

Kitchen grease and dust cut cooling capacity quickly.

Fan failure

Failed fans limit the cold air reaching the rail and cabinet.

Electrical component wear

Start parts weaken over time and stop the compressor from starting.

Gasket and lid wear

Leaks around lids and doors let warm air in and cause frost.
