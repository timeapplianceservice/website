Service Overview

What to Expect From Condensing Unit Repair

The condensing unit is the heart of a walk-in or remote system, and when it struggles the entire box warms. We diagnose compressors, fans, controls and refrigerant pressures to find the cause.

What a visit covers

  • Refrigerant pressure assessment
  • Compressor amp draw and electrical test
  • Crankcase heater and head pressure control check
  • High and low pressure control testing
  • Condenser coil and fan motor check
  • Contactor, capacitor and relay inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Condensing unit repair in Stallings

  • Loud humming or grinding
  • High head pressure
  • Hot compressor housing
  • Frost on suction line
  • Condenser fan not spinning
  • Compressor cutting out on overload
  • Unit running but box warming
  • Unit not starting
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Fan motor failure

A stopped condenser fan leads to high pressure and shutdowns.

Refrigerant circuit problems

Low charge or restrictions reduce cooling and stress the compressor.

Control and pressure faults

Faulty pressure switches or controls shut the unit down.

Compressor issues

Overheating, electrical faults or mechanical wear can take a compressor out of service.
