What to Expect From Evaporator Coil and Fan Repair

When an evaporator ices, the real culprit is usually defrost, airflow or door leaks. We trace it back to the source and repair it.

What a visit covers

  • Coil condition and ice pattern inspection
  • Defrost heater, timer and termination test

Signs You Need Evaporator coil and fan repair in Stallings

  • Fan blades hitting ice
  • Frozen drain pan
  • Short defrost cycles
  • Box not reaching temperature
  • Defrost never ending
  • Frost pattern on one side only

  • Unit alarming for high temperature
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Low airflow from product loading

Crowded shelving blocks air and promotes icing.

Drain problems

Clogged or frozen drains refreeze meltwater on the coil.

Failed defrost components

Heaters, timers and sensors that stop working let ice accumulate.

Door leaks

Humid air entering the box continually frosts the coil.
