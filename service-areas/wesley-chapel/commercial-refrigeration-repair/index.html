Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Defrost system problems

A failed defrost heater, timer or termination sensor lets ice build until airflow is blocked.

Dirty condenser coil

Grease and dust restrict airflow across the condenser, which raises head pressure and starves the system of cooling capacity.

Failed fan motor

A condenser or evaporator fan that stops turning quickly causes warm temperatures, icing or compressor overheating.

Electrical component failure

Capacitors, contactors, relays and start components are common wear items that keep the compressor from starting.
