Service Overview

What to Expect From AC Repair

When the AC struggles on a hot afternoon, the cause is usually a single failed part, not the whole system. We test the electrical, airflow and cooling sides in a logical order and repair what is actually wrong.

What a visit covers

  • Evaporator and condenser coil condition check
  • Condensate drain and pan inspection
  • Capacitor, contactor and relay testing
  • Written findings and repair estimate
  • Indoor blower and outdoor fan motor inspection
  • Thermostat and control check

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Failed capacitor

A weak capacitor keeps the compressor or fan from starting, and the outdoor unit hums.

Fan motor failure

A stalled outdoor or indoor fan reduces heat transfer and can freeze the coil.

Thermostat or control problems

Wiring, sensors and settings can stop the system from responding correctly.

Worn contactor

Pitted contacts cause intermittent starting or a unit that will not run.
