Service Overview

What to Expect From Display Case Repair

From a bakery grab-and-go case to a deli or convenience-store cooler, display cases depend on airflow and defrost cycles that are easy to disturb. We trace issues to the actual component.

What a visit covers

  • Lighting and ballast testing
  • Case temperature and product temperature check
  • Defrost cycle and drain check
  • Fan motor and airflow inspection
  • Condensing unit electrical test
  • Glass, gasket and door seal inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need Display case repair in Weddington

  • Condensation or fog on the glass
  • Product freezing in spots
  • Door not sealing
  • Case not cold enough
  • Lights flickering or out
  • Alarm or controller error
  • Warm spots in the case
  • Water leaking at the base
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Fan motor failure

Evaporator or condenser fans that stop can cause warm temperatures and ice.

Airflow disruption

Overstocked shelves or blocked return vents prevent the case from circulating cold air.

Lighting faults

Failed ballasts and lamps affect product presentation.

Defrost problems

Defrost components that fail leave ice on the coil and block cooling.
