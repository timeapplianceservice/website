Service Overview

What to Expect From Heat Pump Repair

Heat pumps depend on defrost cycles, reversing valves, fans and electrical parts working together. We check each one and repair what is out of spec.

What a visit covers

  • Indoor blower and coil inspection
  • Mode and thermostat control check
  • Reversing valve and defrost control testing
  • Airflow and filter check
  • Written findings and repair estimate
  • Outdoor fan motor, capacitor and contactor testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Heat pump repair in Mint Hill

  • Blowing cool air in heat mode
  • Unit stuck in cooling mode
  • Auxiliary heat running constantly
  • Water or ice around the base
  • Noisy outdoor unit
  • Short cycling
  • Outdoor unit iced over
  • Heat pump not turning on
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Control or wiring faults

Thermostat and board issues can confuse operating modes.

Defrost control problems

A failed defrost board or sensor lets ice build on the outdoor coil.

Reversing valve issues

A stuck valve can leave the unit in the wrong mode.

Weak capacitor or contactor

Electrical wear prevents fans or the compressor from running.
