Service Overview

What to Expect From Reach-In Freezer Repair

Reach-in freezers rely on a working defrost cycle, solid gaskets and clean coils. When any of those slip, the unit ices up and the box warms.

What a visit covers

  • Evaporator and fan motor inspection
  • Drain heater and line inspection
  • Gasket and door closure check
  • Defrost heater, timer and termination test
  • Condenser coil and fan testing
  • Interior and product temperature check
Symptoms

Signs You Need Reach-in freezer repair in Mint Hill

  • Door gasket damaged or warped
  • Excess frost on door and frame
  • Water pooling on the floor
  • Heavy frost on interior walls
  • Compressor clicking but not starting
  • Frozen drain line
  • Unit running without cooling
  • Ice blocking the evaporator fan
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Door seal leaks

Warm air entering the box creates frost and makes the unit run constantly.

Evaporator fan not running

Without a working fan, temperatures climb and the coil ices over.

Dirty condenser

A clogged condenser raises pressures and cuts the unit's freezing capacity.

Refrigerant circuit problems

A leak or restriction lowers capacity and prevents the box from reaching set point.
