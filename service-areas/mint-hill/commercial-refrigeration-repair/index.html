  • Evaporator or condenser fans not turning

  • Water pooling in or under the unit

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Refrigerant leak or restriction

Low charge or a restricted metering device reduces cooling and shows up as long run times and warm product.

Failed fan motor

A condenser or evaporator fan that stops turning quickly causes warm temperatures, icing or compressor overheating.

Worn door gaskets and hardware

Leaking gaskets and sagging doors let warm, moist air in, which overworks the system and frosts the coil.

Electrical component failure

Capacitors, contactors, relays and start components are common wear items that keep the compressor from starting.
