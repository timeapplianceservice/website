What to Expect From Reach-In Cooler Repair

Reach-in refrigerators carry the day-to-day load on the line, which makes them one of the most-opened pieces of equipment in a kitchen. We fix the cause of warm temperatures rather than resetting a control and hoping.

Symptoms

Signs You Need Reach-in cooler repair in Mint Hill

  • Controller showing an error

  • Compressor running all the time
  • Doors sagging or not closing

  • Interior warmer than 41°F
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Dirty condenser coil

Reach-ins sit near grease and heat, and a clogged condenser quickly pushes temperatures up.

Clogged drain

A blocked drain tube sends condensate to the floor or back into the cabinet.

Evaporator fan failure

A seized fan stops air movement, causing warm spots and eventually a frozen coil.

Door gasket failure

A loose or torn gasket leaks cold air, frosts the coil and keeps the compressor running.
