Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Door and seal damage

Doors left ajar or gaskets damaged cause fast temperature loss.

Fan failures

A stopped fan can cause quick temperature rise or icing.

Refrigerant leaks

Loss of charge leads to rapid warming and long run times.

Electrical failures

Contactors, capacitors and relays are common causes of sudden shutdowns.
