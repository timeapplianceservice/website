Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Failed fan motors

A stopped fan leaves warm spots or lets the coil ice over.

Restricted airflow

Tight cabinet placement and dust-packed condensers choke airflow and raise temperatures.

Worn gaskets

Cracked gaskets leak cold air and force the compressor to run constantly.

Control or sensor failure

A faulty thermostat or sensor can cause freezing or warm temperatures.
