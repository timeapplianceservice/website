Service Overview

What to Expect From HVAC Diagnostics

Guessing at an HVAC problem wastes money. A diagnostic visit measures, tests and traces the problem so the repair targets the actual failure.

What a visit covers

  • Airflow and temperature measurements
  • Electrical measurements on key components
  • Written findings and repair estimate
  • Thermostat and control testing
  • Interview about symptoms and history
  • Visual inspection of coils, drains and motors

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Mechanical wear

Moving parts wear with age and use.

Electrical faults

Capacitors, contactors, relays and wiring are the most common failures.

Control issues

Thermostat and board faults can mimic equipment failures.

Drain and moisture problems

Clogged drains can shut down the system or leak.
