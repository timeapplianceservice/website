What to Expect From Walk-In Cooler Repair

A walk-in cooler holds most of a restaurant's inventory, so a few degrees of drift can put thousands of dollars of product at risk. We track the problem from the controller to the evaporator to the condensing unit until we find the real cause.

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Evaporator fan failure

Without air movement the coil freezes and the box warms, often with a visible block of ice.

Condenser airflow problems

A dirty coil or failed fan raises pressure and cuts capacity, so the box cannot reach set point.

Iced evaporator coil

Failed defrost components or a door left ajar load the evaporator with frost until air can no longer move through the coil.

Failed start or run components

Contactors and capacitors wear from heavy cycling and can stop the compressor from starting.
