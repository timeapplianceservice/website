Service Overview

What to Expect From Ice Machine Repair

Modular heads, undercounter and self-contained ice machines all share the same needs: clean water, clean condensers and working sensors. We service each part of that chain.

What a visit covers

  • Evaporator, harvest and water pump inspection
  • Sanitizing and descaling guidance
  • Water supply, filter and inlet valve check
  • Float, sensor and bin control testing
  • Refrigeration pressure assessment
  • Condenser airflow and cleaning check
Symptoms

Signs You Need Ice machine repair in Ballantyne

  • Machine running but not harvesting
  • Ice sticking together
  • Off taste or odor in ice
  • Cloudy, slushy or hollow ice
  • Machine shuts off before bin is full
  • Low ice production
  • Bin not filling
  • Unusual noises
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Refrigeration faults

Compressor or refrigerant circuit issues reduce capacity.

Water supply issues

A clogged filter or failing inlet valve starves the machine of water.

Water pump or distribution

A weak pump or clogged distribution tube leaves partial ice sheets.

Harvest and sensor faults

Bad thermistors, probes or bin controls prevent proper harvest or shut-off.
