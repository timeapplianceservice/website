What to Expect From HVAC Maintenance

Regular maintenance is the best way to prevent a mid-summer breakdown. We clean, test and measure the parts that wear most, and give you a clear report on what we find.

What a visit covers

  • Electrical connection and capacitor testing
  • Thermostat and control check
  • Condensate drain flush
  • Coil and filter condition review
  • Temperature and airflow measurements
  • Blower and fan motor inspection

Signs You Need HVAC maintenance in Ballantyne

  • Musty air from the vents
  • Frequent filter clogging

  • Moving into a home with unknown HVAC history

Electrical part wear

Control and sensor issues
