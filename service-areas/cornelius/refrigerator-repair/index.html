Service Overview

What to Expect From Refrigerator Repair

A refrigerator that stops cooling puts a week of groceries at risk, so speed and accuracy matter. We test the sealed system, fans, defrost components and controls to find the real cause before recommending a repair.

What a visit covers

  • Control board and sensor diagnostics
  • Evaporator and condenser fan inspection
  • Defrost heater, sensor and timer testing
  • Door gasket and hinge inspection
  • Condenser coil and airflow check
  • Temperature check in the fresh-food and freezer sections
Symptoms

Signs You Need Refrigerator repair in Cornelius

  • Freezer fine but fridge warm
  • Refrigerator not cooling
  • Compressor running constantly
  • Ice maker not working
  • Loud buzzing or clicking
  • Display showing an error
  • Water leaking inside or under the unit
  • Ice build-up in the freezer
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Control board or sensor issues

Electronic faults can stop cooling or cause temperature swings.

Failed start relay or capacitor

These parts keep the compressor from starting.

Defrost system faults

A failed heater, sensor or timer lets ice block airflow.

Bad door gasket

A leaking seal lets warm air in and frost build.
