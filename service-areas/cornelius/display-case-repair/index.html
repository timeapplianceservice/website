What to Expect From Display Case Repair

Display cases do double duty, keeping product cold and keeping it appealing. We diagnose temperature, airflow, defrost and lighting problems so the case looks good and performs.

What a visit covers

  • Defrost cycle and drain check
  • Case temperature and product temperature check
  • Lighting and ballast testing

Symptoms

Signs You Need Display case repair in Cornelius

  • Compressor running constantly
  • Water leaking at the base
  • Lights flickering or out
  • Condensation or fog on the glass
  • Warm spots in the case
  • Door not sealing
  • Ice building on the evaporator
  • Case not cold enough
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Gasket or door leaks

Leaking seals invite warm air and condensation.

Condensing unit issues

Failed capacitors, contactors or compressors leave the case without cooling.

Defrost problems

Defrost components that fail leave ice on the coil and block cooling.

Airflow disruption

Overstocked shelves or blocked return vents prevent the case from circulating cold air.
