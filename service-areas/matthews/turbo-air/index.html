Common Turbo Air Problems

Dirty condenser coils

Kitchen grease and dust cut cooling capacity.

Thermostat and controller faults

Bad sensors cause warm or frozen product.

Fan motor failure

Evaporator and condenser fans stall from heat and wear.

Door gasket wear

Leaking gaskets cause frost and constant running.

Clogged drain lines

Condensate backs up and drips.

Refrigerant circuit issues

Leaks or restrictions reduce capacity.
