Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Dirty blower wheel

Dust on the wheel reduces airflow and throws it off balance.

Control module faults

Variable-speed motors depend on a working control module.

Overheating

Heat and strain shorten motor life.

Restricted airflow

Clogged filters and coils make the motor work harder.
