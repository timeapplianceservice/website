Service Overview

What to Expect From Oven Repair

An oven that will not hold temperature can ruin dinner and disrupt your week. We diagnose heating elements, igniters, sensors and controls and repair the cause.

What a visit covers

  • Control board and display diagnostics
  • Oven calibration
  • Convection fan check
  • Door latch and lock inspection
  • Temperature sensor check
  • Bake and broil element or igniter testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Oven repair in Matthews

  • Uneven baking
  • Oven too hot or too cold
  • Door will not lock
  • Display showing an error
  • Oven light out
  • Igniter glowing but no flame
  • Self-clean not working
  • Broil not working
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Failed igniter

On gas ovens a weak igniter prevents the gas valve from opening.

Bad bake or broil element

Electric ovens lose heat when elements burn out.

Faulty temperature sensor

Wrong readings cause temperature problems.

Convection fan failure

Uneven heating and slow baking follow.
