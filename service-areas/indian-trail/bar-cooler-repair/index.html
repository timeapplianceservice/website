Signs You Need Bar cooler repair in Indian Trail

  • Glass doors fogging or frosting

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Heat and airflow

Tight installations and hot bar environments push condensers beyond their limits.

Defrost or sensor faults

Ice and wrong readings follow control problems.

Electrical wear

Start parts and relays weaken from repeated cycling.

Dirty condenser

Dust and bar debris reduce cooling capacity.
