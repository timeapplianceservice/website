Common Causes We Find

Power quality

Voltage drops and surges can damage electrical parts.

Weak run capacitor

Capacitors lose capacity from heat and age, and cannot help the motor start.

Compressor strain

Hard starts can stress the compressor.

Loose wiring

Heat and vibration loosen terminals.
