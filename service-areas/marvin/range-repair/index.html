Service Overview

What to Expect From Range Repair

The range is the heart of the kitchen, and when a burner or oven stops working it affects every meal. We diagnose igniters, valves, elements and controls and repair the failure.

What a visit covers

  • Control board diagnostics
  • Burner and igniter inspection
  • Oven sensor and thermostat check
  • Electric element and infinite switch testing
  • Safety check
  • Gas valve and spark module testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Range repair in Marvin

  • Gas smell (leave the home and call your gas utility first)
  • Weak or uneven flame
  • Oven too hot or cold
  • Knobs not working
  • Clicking without ignition
  • Element not heating
  • Burner stays on
  • Display error
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Failed spark module

The ignition system stops sparking.

Gas valve faults

Burners do not light or run properly.

Dirty or failed igniter

Burners will not light or click continuously.

Oven sensor problems

Temperatures are wrong.
