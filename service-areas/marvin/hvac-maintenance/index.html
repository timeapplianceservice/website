Service Overview

What to Expect From HVAC Maintenance

A tune-up covers the electrical parts, airflow, coils and drains that cause most HVAC failures. Early attention to a weak capacitor or dirty coil is far cheaper than an emergency call.

What a visit covers

  • Thermostat and control check
  • Blower and fan motor inspection
  • Condensate drain flush
  • Coil and filter condition review
  • Electrical connection and capacitor testing
  • Temperature and airflow measurements
Symptoms

Signs You Need HVAC maintenance in Marvin

  • Uneven temperatures between rooms
  • Frequent filter clogging
  • Longer run times than before
  • Desire to avoid a breakdown
  • Older system that still runs
  • Preparing for summer or winter
  • Musty air from the vents
  • Moving into a home with unknown HVAC history
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Dirty coils and filters

Dirt reduces airflow and efficiency and can lead to freezing.

Airflow problems

Poor airflow stresses equipment and affects comfort.

Clogged drains

Drain lines clog with algae and debris, and flushing helps prevent water damage.

Motor and bearing wear

Early detection avoids unexpected failures.
