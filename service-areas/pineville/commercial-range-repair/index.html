Service Overview

What to Expect From Commercial Range Repair

Heavy use, boil-overs and grease take a toll on commercial ranges. We clean, test and repair the parts that fail, and explain what caused the failure.

What a visit covers

  • Electric element and contactor testing
  • Oven thermostat and ignition checks
  • Safety device verification
  • Knob, switch and control inspection
  • Burner and pilot inspection
  • Gas valve and thermocouple testing on gas ranges
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial range repair in Pineville

  • Strong gas odor (leave the area and call your gas utility first)
  • Yellow or lazy flame
  • Oven not heating to temperature
  • Oven door not sealing
  • Control knobs stuck or loose
  • Burner will not light
  • Pilot keeps going out
  • Weak or uneven flame
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Gas valve problems

A sticking or failing valve causes burners not to light or to run wrong.

Faulty pilot or thermocouple

A worn thermocouple or dirty pilot lets the pilot go out and the valve shut.

Oven thermostat failure

A bad thermostat misreads temperature and leads to under or overheating.

Electric element or switch failure

Elements and infinite switches wear out from heat and cycling.
