Service Overview

What to Expect From Commercial Dishwasher Repair

Dishmachines run continuously in hot, wet conditions. We service the components that wear fastest, including heating elements, pumps, solenoids and wash arms.

What a visit covers

  • Heating element and booster check
  • Wash pump and arm inspection
  • Drain and fill solenoid testing
  • Door switch and control checks
  • Wash and rinse temperature verification
  • Chemical feed and sanitizer verification
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial dishwasher repair in Pineville

  • Not filling with water
  • Error code
  • Not reaching proper temperature
  • Detergent or rinse aid not feeding
  • Leaking at the door or base
  • Strange noises from the pump
  • Wash arms not spinning
  • Dishes coming out dirty or spotty
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Clogged wash arms or jets

Debris and scale reduce spray action.

Pump or seal wear

Leaks and noise often come from worn seals and pumps.

Heating element or booster failure

Water that does not reach required temperatures causes poor cleaning and sanitizing.

Drain or fill problems

Failed solenoids or clogged drains disrupt the cycle.
