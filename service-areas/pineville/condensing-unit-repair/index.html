Service Overview

What to Expect From Condensing Unit Repair

Indoor and outdoor condensing units fail from heat, dirt and electrical wear. We check each of these and repair the failing component.

What a visit covers

  • Compressor amp draw and electrical test
  • Crankcase heater and head pressure control check
  • Condenser coil and fan motor check
  • High and low pressure control testing
  • Contactor, capacitor and relay inspection
  • Refrigerant pressure assessment

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Compressor issues

Overheating, electrical faults or mechanical wear can take a compressor out of service.

Control and pressure faults

Faulty pressure switches or controls shut the unit down.

Dirty condenser coil

Restricted airflow raises pressure and overheats the compressor.

Refrigerant circuit problems

Low charge or restrictions reduce cooling and stress the compressor.
