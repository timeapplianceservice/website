Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Drain and moisture problems

Clogged drains can shut down the system or leak.

Motor and bearing wear

Fan and blower motors are frequent culprits.

Control issues

Thermostat and board faults can mimic equipment failures.

Airflow restrictions

Dirty filters, coils and blower problems affect performance.
