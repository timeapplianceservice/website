What We Do in Pineville

Pineville sits at the southern edge of Mecklenburg County, close to the South Carolina line, and mixes a historic downtown with busy retail along the US-521 and NC-51 corridors.

Pineville is a small town with big-city traffic, home to Carolina Place and a steady stream of restaurants, shops and nearby neighborhoods.

Pineville's historic downtown has small restaurants and bars with older buildings, where compact walk-ins and reach-in units do most of the cold storage work.

Pineville homes range from mid-century houses to newer townhomes, so we regularly see both older and recent appliance models in a single week.

Between the heat of summer and cooler winter nights, Pineville HVAC systems cycle through heavy use, putting wear on contactors, capacitors and fan motors.

Our technicians serve Pineville homeowners throughout Mecklenburg County, including the Polk Street and Johnston Road area areas.
