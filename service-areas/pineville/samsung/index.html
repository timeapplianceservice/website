Diagnosis

Common Samsung Problems

Ice makers and water valves

Fill valves and ice maker modules are common wear items.

Control boards and sensors

Electronic controls fail from heat, moisture and power surges.

Evaporator and condenser fans

Fan motors wear out and cause warm temperatures or noise.

Drain pumps and belts

Washers and dishwashers fail from clogged pumps and worn belts.

Door seals and latches

Worn seals and latches cause leaks, frost and no-start problems.

Heating elements and igniters

Ovens, ranges and dryers lose heat when elements or igniters wear out.
