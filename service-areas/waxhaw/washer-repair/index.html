Service Overview

What to Expect From Washer Repair

A washer that stops mid-cycle can turn into a flooded laundry room, so we diagnose quickly and carefully. We test pumps, motors, bearings, door locks and controls to find the fault.

What a visit covers

  • Suspension and balance check
  • Control board and sensor diagnostics
  • Water inlet valve and filter check
  • Door lock and latch testing
  • Drain pump and hose inspection
  • Drive belt, motor and bearing inspection

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Broken drive belt

Belt-driven washers stop spinning when the belt breaks or slips.

Worn bearings or shocks

They cause loud noise and violent shaking.

Door lock failure

The washer will not start if the door does not lock.

Control board or sensor faults

Electronic problems can stop cycles or show error codes.
