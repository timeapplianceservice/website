Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Defrost system faults

A failed heater, sensor or timer lets ice block airflow.

Failed start relay or capacitor

These parts keep the compressor from starting.

Control board or sensor issues

Electronic faults can stop cooling or cause temperature swings.

Dirty condenser coil

Dust restricts heat release and makes the compressor work harder.
