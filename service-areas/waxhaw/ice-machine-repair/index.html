Service Overview

What to Expect From Ice Machine Repair

Commercial ice machines depend on clean water, proper airflow and healthy refrigeration. We diagnose scale, water flow, harvest and sensor problems and repair what keeps ice production low.

What a visit covers

  • Water supply, filter and inlet valve check
  • Evaporator, harvest and water pump inspection
  • Refrigeration pressure assessment
  • Float, sensor and bin control testing
  • Sanitizing and descaling guidance
  • Condenser airflow and cleaning check

Signs You Need Ice machine repair in Waxhaw

  • Bin not filling

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Refrigeration faults

Compressor or refrigerant circuit issues reduce capacity.

Condenser problems

A dirty condenser or failed fan raises pressures and slows ice making.

Harvest and sensor faults

Bad thermistors, probes or bin controls prevent proper harvest or shut-off.

Scale and water quality

Mineral buildup coats the evaporator and sensors and reduces ice production.
