Service Overview

What to Expect From Washer Repair

Most washer failures involve the drain pump, a belt, the door lock, a water inlet valve or the control board. We check each and repair the one that failed.

What a visit covers

  • Drain pump and hose inspection
  • Drive belt, motor and bearing inspection
  • Door lock and latch testing
  • Water inlet valve and filter check
  • Suspension and balance check
  • Control board and sensor diagnostics
Symptoms

Signs You Need Washer repair in Indian Land

  • Leaking water
  • Loud banging or grinding
  • Washer walking across the floor
  • Washer not draining
  • Door will not unlock
  • Washer not filling
  • Foul odor from the drum
  • Clothes still soaking wet
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Clogged or failed drain pump

Lint and small items block the pump or the motor wears out.

Worn bearings or shocks

They cause loud noise and violent shaking.

Broken drive belt

Belt-driven washers stop spinning when the belt breaks or slips.

Faulty inlet valve

The washer will not fill correctly.
