Service Overview

What to Expect From Range Repair

Gas, electric and dual-fuel ranges each have their own parts, and premium pro-style models have extra features. We match the diagnosis to your range.

What a visit covers

  • Burner and igniter inspection
  • Oven sensor and thermostat check
  • Control board diagnostics
  • Electric element and infinite switch testing
  • Safety check
  • Gas valve and spark module testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need Range repair in Indian Land

  • Weak or uneven flame
  • Door will not lock
  • Display error
  • Oven too hot or cold
  • Gas smell (leave the home and call your gas utility first)
  • Burner will not light
  • Oven not heating
  • Clicking without ignition
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Dirty or failed igniter

Burners will not light or click continuously.

Faulty infinite switch

Electric burners fail to heat or stay on.

Control board issues

Electronic faults cause errors or no heat.

Gas valve faults

Burners do not light or run properly.
