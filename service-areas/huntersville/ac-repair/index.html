Service Overview

What to Expect From AC Repair

Air conditioners fail in familiar ways: weak capacitors, worn contactors, tired fan motors, frozen coils and clogged drains. Our technicians check each of these and explain the findings before any repair begins.

What a visit covers

  • Capacitor, contactor and relay testing
  • Evaporator and condenser coil condition check
  • Written findings and repair estimate
  • Condensate drain and pan inspection
  • Thermostat and control check
  • Indoor blower and outdoor fan motor inspection
Symptoms

Signs You Need AC repair in Huntersville

  • AC running but not cooling
  • Outdoor unit humming but not starting
  • Water leaking near the air handler
  • Breaker tripping when AC starts
  • Unusual noises
  • AC not turning on
  • Ice on the indoor coil or lines
  • Rising energy bills
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Thermostat or control problems

Wiring, sensors and settings can stop the system from responding correctly.

Clogged condensate drain

A blocked drain can shut the system down or leak water.

Fan motor failure

A stalled outdoor or indoor fan reduces heat transfer and can freeze the coil.

Worn contactor

Pitted contacts cause intermittent starting or a unit that will not run.
