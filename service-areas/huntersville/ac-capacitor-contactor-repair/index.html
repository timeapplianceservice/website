Service Overview

What to Expect From AC Capacitor and Contactor Repair

Capacitors and contactors are among the most common reasons an AC will not start. They are small parts that carry a big load, and they wear out from heat and cycling.

What a visit covers

  • Amp draw measurement
  • Wiring and terminal inspection
  • System operation verification
  • Fan and compressor start check
  • Contactor contact and coil inspection
  • Capacitor microfarad testing
Symptoms

Signs You Need AC capacitor repair in Huntersville

  • Breaker tripping
  • Bulging capacitor
  • Contactor chattering
  • Outdoor unit humming without starting
  • AC works after being tapped
  • Burning smell near the outdoor unit
  • Fan needs a push to start
  • AC clicks but will not run
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Weak run capacitor

Capacitors lose capacity from heat and age, and cannot help the motor start.

Loose wiring

Heat and vibration loosen terminals.

Motor issues

Failing fan motors strain the capacitor.

Compressor strain

Hard starts can stress the compressor.
