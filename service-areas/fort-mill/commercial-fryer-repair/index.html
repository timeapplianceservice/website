Service Overview

What to Expect From Commercial Fryer Repair

A fryer that will not hold temperature affects food quality and speed. We repair gas and electric fryers, from pilots and burners to thermostats, high-limit switches and filtration systems.

What a visit covers

  • Basket lift and control checks
  • Filtration pump and valve inspection
  • Thermostat and high-limit testing
  • Heating element and contactor testing (electric fryers)
  • Burner, pilot and gas valve inspection (gas fryers)
  • Oil temperature verification
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial fryer repair in Fort Mill

  • Oil overheating
  • High-limit tripping repeatedly
  • Pilot will not stay lit
  • Excess smoke or burning smell
  • Basket lift not moving
  • Burner lighting slowly or noisily
  • Controller showing an error
  • Filtration pump not working
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Pilot and thermopile problems

Weak thermopiles and dirty pilots keep gas valves from opening.

High-limit switch issues

Tripped or failing limits shut the fryer down for safety.

Control board or contactor failure

Electronic parts can fail from heat and grease.

Heating element failure

Electric elements wear out and stop heating.
