Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Restricted airflow

Tight cabinet placement and dust-packed condensers choke airflow and raise temperatures.

Clogged drain

Blocked condensate lines drip onto the floor or back up into the cabinet.

Worn gaskets

Cracked gaskets leak cold air and force the compressor to run constantly.

Electrical start parts

Weak capacitors and relays can stop the compressor from starting.
