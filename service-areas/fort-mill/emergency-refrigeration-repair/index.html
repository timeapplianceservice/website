Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Power events

Outages and surges can damage controls and compressors.

Refrigerant leaks

Loss of charge leads to rapid warming and long run times.

Compressor problems

An overheated or failed compressor can stop cooling entirely.

Electrical failures

Contactors, capacitors and relays are common causes of sudden shutdowns.
