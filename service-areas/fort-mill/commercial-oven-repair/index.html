Service Overview

What to Expect From Commercial Oven Repair

Your ovens set the pace of the line, so a unit that will not hold temperature slows every order. We diagnose gas and electric commercial ovens, from ignition and thermostats to fans, doors and control boards.

What a visit covers

  • Control board and sensor diagnostics
  • Temperature calibration and thermostat testing
  • Heating element and contactor testing on electric models
  • Convection fan motor and bearing inspection
  • Ignition, pilot and gas valve checks on gas models
  • Door gasket, hinge and latch check
Symptoms

Signs You Need Commercial oven repair in Fort Mill

  • Convection fan noisy or stopped
  • Oven shutting off during service
  • Uneven baking or hot spots
  • Pilot or igniter not lighting
  • Burning smell or smoke
  • Gas smell (leave the area and call your gas utility first)
  • Steam or water issues on combi models
  • Oven not heating or slow to heat
Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Convection fan problems

Worn bearings and failed motors cause uneven heat distribution.

Door gasket or latch wear

Leaking doors lose heat and slow recovery.

Control board faults

Electronic controls can fail from heat and grease, showing errors or shutting down.

Faulty thermostat or temperature probe

A drifting sensor leads to under or overheating and inconsistent results.
