  • Filtration pump and valve inspection

Common Causes We Find

Control board or contactor failure

Electronic parts can fail from heat and grease.

Heating element failure

Electric elements wear out and stop heating.

Pilot and thermopile problems

Weak thermopiles and dirty pilots keep gas valves from opening.

Filtration system faults

Clogged filters and weak pumps interrupt oil management.
