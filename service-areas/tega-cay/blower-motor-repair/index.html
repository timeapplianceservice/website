Service Overview

What to Expect From Blower Motor Repair

The blower moves air through your whole house, so when it fails the system cannot heat or cool. We test the motor, capacitor and control to find what is limiting airflow.

What a visit covers

  • Capacitor testing
  • Motor amp draw and bearing check
  • Blower wheel inspection
  • Airflow measurements
  • Control module and wiring check
  • Filter and coil restriction check

Diagnosis

Common Causes We Find

Restricted airflow

Clogged filters and coils make the motor work harder.

Worn bearings

Aging motors develop noise and eventually seize.

Failed capacitor

A weak capacitor prevents the motor from starting or running at speed.

Overheating

Heat and strain shorten motor life.
