Diagnosis

Common Fisher & Paykel Problems

Ice maker and water system problems

Water valves, filters and lines are common failure points on premium refrigeration.

Fans and defrost parts

Evaporator fans and defrost components are frequent causes of warm temperatures.

Control board and sensor faults

High-end models rely on electronic controls, and a failing sensor or board can mimic a bigger problem.

Door seals and hinges

Tight tolerances mean worn seals show up quickly as temperature or sealing problems.

Error codes

Brand-specific codes point to the fault, and we interpret them and verify with tests.

Parts availability

Premium parts can have lead times, so we explain timing before ordering.
