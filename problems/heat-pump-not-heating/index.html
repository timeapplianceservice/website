Overview

Why Heat Pump Not Heating Happens

Heat Pump Not Heating is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as defrost control problems, reversing valve trouble or weak capacitor or contactor. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Cool air in heat mode
  • Outdoor unit iced over
  • Auxiliary heat running constantly
  • Unit not turning on
  • High winter energy bills
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Set the thermostat to heat
  • Check the filter
  • See if the outdoor unit is running or iced
  • Note whether auxiliary heat is on

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Defrost control problems

Ice builds on the outdoor coil.

Reversing valve trouble

The unit stays in the wrong mode.

Weak capacitor or contactor

The outdoor unit cannot run properly.

Outdoor fan failure

Heat transfer is lost.

Airflow restrictions

Dirty filters or coils limit capacity.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check mode and controls
  • Inspect outdoor coil and fan
  • Test electrical parts and defrost
  • Measure temperatures and pressures

Heat pumps depend on defrost cycles, reversing valves, fans and electrical parts working together. We check each one and repair what is out of spec.
