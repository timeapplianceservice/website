Overview

Why Condensing Unit High Head Pressure Happens

Condensing Unit High Head Pressure is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as dirty condenser, failed fan motor or overcharge or non-condensables. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Unit shutting off on high pressure
  • Hot discharge line
  • Condenser fan slow or stopped
  • Coil caked with dirt
  • Compressor overheating
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check for dirt on the condenser
  • Make sure fans spin freely
  • Ensure airflow is not blocked
  • Note ambient temperature when it trips

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Dirty condenser

Restricted airflow raises pressure.

Failed fan motor

Heat cannot be rejected.

Overcharge or non-condensables

System charge issues raise pressure.

Blocked airflow

Walls, debris or recirculation choke the unit.

Control faults

Fan cycling controls may misbehave.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Measure pressures and temperatures
  • Inspect coil and fans
  • Test fan controls
  • Check refrigerant system condition

Indoor and outdoor condensing units fail from heat, dirt and electrical wear. We check each of these and repair the failing component.
