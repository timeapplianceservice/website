Overview

Why Dishwasher Not Cleaning Happens

When you are dealing with dishwasher not cleaning, the cause is rarely the whole dishwasher. It is usually one failed part, most often clogged spray arms, weak pump or heating failure. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Dirty dishes
  • Residue on glassware
  • Gritty film
  • Spray arm not moving
  • Detergent left in the dispenser
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Clean the filter
  • Check spray arms
  • Use fresh detergent
  • Run hot water before starting

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Clogged spray arms

Spray is blocked.

Weak pump

Water pressure is low.

Heating failure

Water is not hot.

Dispenser faults

Detergent is not released.

Filter buildup

Debris recirculates.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check spray arms
  • Test pump and heater
  • Check dispenser
  • Clean filter

Modern dishwashers rely on sensors, pumps and control boards in addition to heaters and spray arms. We test each part to find why the cycle fails.
