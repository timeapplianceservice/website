Overview

Why Refrigerator Leaking Water Happens

When you are dealing with refrigerator leaking water, the cause is rarely the whole refrigerator. It is usually one failed part, most often clogged defrost drain, loose water line or failed inlet valve. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Puddle under the fridge
  • Water inside the crisper
  • Ice in the bottom of the freezer
  • Dripping from the dispenser
  • Wet floor near the fridge
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the water line connection
  • Clear the defrost drain
  • Check the drip pan
  • Look for ice on the back wall

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Clogged defrost drain

Water backs up and leaks.

Loose water line

Fittings leak.

Failed inlet valve

Water leaks into the line.

Cracked drip pan

Water escapes.

Ice maker problems

Overflow leaks to the floor.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Trace the water
  • Inspect drain and line
  • Test valve
  • Check ice maker

Most refrigerator problems come down to a handful of parts: fans, defrost components, sensors, door seals and control boards. We check them in a logical order and explain what we find.
