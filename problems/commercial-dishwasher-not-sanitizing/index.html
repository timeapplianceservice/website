Overview

Why Commercial Dishwasher Not Sanitizing Happens

Commercial Dishwasher Not Sanitizing is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as heating element failure, booster heater problems or chemical feed faults. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Water not hot enough
  • Final rinse low
  • Chemical level low
  • Dishes not sanitized
  • Test strips fail
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check incoming water temperature
  • Check chemical levels
  • Verify rinse temperature gauge
  • Clean wash arms

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Heating element failure

Water does not reach temperature.

Booster heater problems

Final rinse is too cool.

Chemical feed faults

Sanitizer is not delivered.

Clogged spray arms

Rinse coverage drops.

Thermostat or sensor faults

Controls misread temperature.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Verify wash and rinse temperatures
  • Test heating and booster
  • Check chemical feed
  • Inspect spray arms

High-temp and low-temp commercial dishwashers each have different parts and checks. We test the machine's water temperature, wash action and chemical delivery.
