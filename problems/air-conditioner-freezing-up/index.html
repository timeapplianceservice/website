Overview

Why Air Conditioner Freezing Up Happens

When you are dealing with air conditioner freezing up, the cause is rarely the whole air conditioner. It is usually one failed part, most often restricted airflow, blower motor trouble or low refrigerant charge. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Ice on the indoor coil
  • Frost on the refrigerant line
  • Weak airflow
  • Water dripping when it thaws
  • AC not cooling
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Turn the AC off and set the fan to on to thaw the coil
  • Check and clean the filter
  • Make sure vents are open
  • Avoid chipping ice

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Restricted airflow

Dirty filters, coils or blocked vents allow ice.

Blower motor trouble

Reduced airflow lets the coil freeze.

Low refrigerant charge

Pressure drops and the coil freezes.

Dirty evaporator coil

Dirt insulates and restricts airflow.

Thermostat or fan settings

Incorrect settings stop proper airflow.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Thaw and inspect the coil
  • Check airflow and blower
  • Check filter and returns
  • Measure pressures and temperatures

Air conditioners fail in familiar ways: weak capacitors, worn contactors, tired fan motors, frozen coils and clogged drains. Our technicians check each of these and explain the findings before any repair begins.
