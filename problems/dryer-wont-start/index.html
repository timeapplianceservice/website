Overview

Why Dryer Won't Start Happens

When you are dealing with dryer won't start, the cause is rarely the whole dryer. It is usually one failed part, most often door switch failure, thermal fuse blown or motor or start switch faults. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No response
  • Hum but no tumble
  • Door switch issues
  • Start button dead
  • Breaker tripped
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the breaker
  • Check the door
  • Check the outlet
  • Try a different cycle

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Door switch failure

The dryer will not start without it.

Thermal fuse blown

The dryer is dead.

Motor or start switch faults

The motor does not start.

Control faults

The board does not respond.

Power issues

Power is not reaching the dryer.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check power and breaker
  • Test door switch
  • Test fuse and motor
  • Check controls

Gas and electric dryers fail in different ways, but airflow problems are involved in many calls. We check the vent along with the dryer to keep operation safe.
