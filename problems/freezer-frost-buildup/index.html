Overview

Why Freezer Frost Buildup Happens

Freezer Frost Buildup can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are door seal leaks, defrost failure and door left ajar, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Frost on walls
  • Ice on the evaporator
  • Frost on the gasket
  • Ice on food
  • Door sticking
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the door seal
  • Make sure the door closes fully
  • Do not leave the door open
  • Let a technician check defrost

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Door seal leaks

Humid air enters.

Defrost failure

Frost is not cleared.

Door left ajar

Warm air enters the freezer.

Drain issues

Water refreezes.

Humid environment

Moist air accelerates frost.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Inspect gasket and door
  • Test defrost heater
  • Check drain
  • Check humidity sources

Home freezers rely on a sealed system, fans and a defrost cycle that are easy to disturb. We test each in turn and explain our findings plainly.
