Overview

Why Air Conditioner Leaking Water Happens

When you are dealing with air conditioner leaking water, the cause is rarely the whole condensate drain. It is usually one failed part, most often clogged condensate drain, cracked drain pan or frozen evaporator coil. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Water around the indoor unit
  • Ceiling stains
  • Overflowing drain pan
  • Musty smell
  • AC shuts off unexpectedly
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Turn the AC off to stop water production
  • Check for a clogged drain line
  • Look for ice on the coil
  • Wipe up standing water

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Clogged condensate drain

Algae and debris block drainage.

Cracked drain pan

Old pans leak.

Frozen evaporator coil

Melting ice overwhelms the drain.

Float switch trips

Water backs up and the safety shuts the system.

Condensate pump failure

Water is not moved away.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Trace the water source
  • Clear and flush the drain
  • Inspect pan and pump
  • Check coil and airflow

Algae, dirt and debris build up inside condensate lines, especially in humid climates like ours. We clear the line, inspect the pan and test the safety switch.
