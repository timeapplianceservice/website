Overview

Why Reach-In Freezer Not Freezing Happens

When you are dealing with reach-in freezer not freezing, the cause is rarely the whole reach-in freezer. It is usually one failed part, most often defrost failure, fan failure or gasket leaks. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Food safety: if product has been above 41°F for an extended period, move perishable items to working refrigeration and follow your local health department guidance.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Freezer above 0°F
  • Frost building heavily
  • Fan stalled
  • Compressor clicking
  • Product soft
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the door seal
  • Look for frost on the evaporator
  • Make sure vents are clear
  • Check the control setting

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Defrost failure

Ice blocks the coil.

Fan failure

No air movement.

Gasket leaks

Warm air enters.

Dirty condenser

Poor heat release.

Electrical start parts

Compressor does not start.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Test defrost system
  • Check fans
  • Test compressor start components
  • Inspect gaskets and condenser

Reach-in freezers rely on a working defrost cycle, solid gaskets and clean coils. When any of those slip, the unit ices up and the box warms.
