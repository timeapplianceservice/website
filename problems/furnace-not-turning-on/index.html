Overview

Why Furnace Not Turning On Happens

When you are dealing with furnace not turning on, the cause is rarely the whole furnace. It is usually one failed part, most often tripped breaker or switch, blown fuse or transformer problem or thermostat fault. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No response at all
  • Blower will not start
  • Thermostat calling but no action
  • Error code
  • Clicking without ignition
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the breaker and furnace switch
  • Check thermostat batteries
  • Check for a closed gas valve
  • Check the filter and panel switch

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Tripped breaker or switch

Power is cut.

Blown fuse or transformer problem

The control board has no power.

Thermostat fault

The heat call is not made.

Door or limit switch open

Safety circuits prevent start.

Control board issues

The board does not run the sequence.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Verify power and control voltage
  • Test thermostat and wiring
  • Check safety switches
  • Test board and components

Furnaces rely on a chain of parts, including the thermostat, inducer, igniter, flame sensor and blower. We follow that chain until we find the break and repair it.
