Overview

Why Dryer Taking Too Long to Dry Happens

Dryer Taking Too Long to Dry can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are clogged vent, weak heat and blower issues, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Multiple cycles needed
  • Clothes damp
  • Dryer hot to the touch
  • Lint on clothes
  • Vent hot
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Clean the lint filter
  • Check the vent and duct
  • Do not overload
  • Check the exterior vent flap

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Clogged vent

Airflow is restricted.

Weak heat

Elements or igniters are failing.

Blower issues

Airflow is reduced.

Thermostat faults

Heat cycles incorrectly.

Moisture sensor faults

The dryer ends cycles early.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check vent airflow
  • Test heating
  • Check blower
  • Check sensors

Gas and electric dryers fail in different ways, but airflow problems are involved in many calls. We check the vent along with the dryer to keep operation safe.
