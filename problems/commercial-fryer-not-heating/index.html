Overview

Why Commercial Fryer Not Heating Happens

Commercial Fryer Not Heating is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as failed thermostat or probe, pilot or thermopile problems or high-limit switch trips. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Oil not heating
  • Slow recovery
  • Pilot out
  • High-limit tripping
  • Controller error
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the gas supply and breaker
  • Check the reset button
  • Make sure the oil level is correct
  • If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Failed thermostat or probe

The fryer cannot regulate temperature.

Pilot or thermopile problems

The gas valve will not open.

High-limit switch trips

The safety circuit opens.

Heating element failure

Electric fryers lose heat.

Control faults

Controls fail.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Verify power or gas
  • Test probe and thermostat
  • Check high-limit
  • Inspect burner or elements

Fryers work in a harsh environment of heat, oil and moisture. Components wear quickly, and small problems become safety concerns if ignored.
