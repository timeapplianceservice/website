Overview

Why Range Burner Won't Light Happens

When you are dealing with range burner won't light, the cause is rarely the whole range. It is usually one failed part, most often dirty igniter, wet burner or spark module failure. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Clicking
  • Burner will not light
  • Weak flame
  • Burner stays on
  • Gas smell
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Clean the burner and igniter
  • Make sure parts are seated
  • Dry the igniter
  • If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Dirty igniter

Spark is weak.

Wet burner

Moisture blocks ignition.

Spark module failure

No spark.

Clogged ports

Flame is uneven.

Gas valve issues

Gas does not flow.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Inspect burner and igniter
  • Test spark module
  • Check ports
  • Test valve

Gas, electric and dual-fuel ranges each have their own parts, and premium pro-style models have extra features. We match the diagnosis to your range.
