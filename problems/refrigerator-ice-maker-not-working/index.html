Overview

Why Refrigerator Ice Maker Not Working Happens

Refrigerator Ice Maker Not Working is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as frozen fill line, failed inlet valve or clogged filter. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No ice
  • Small cubes
  • Ice stuck together
  • Leaking
  • Dispenser jammed
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the water line
  • Check the freezer temperature
  • Check the filter
  • Make sure the ice maker is on

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Frozen fill line

Water cannot reach the maker.

Failed inlet valve

No water flows.

Clogged filter

Flow is low.

Heater or motor failure

Ice cannot release.

Sensor faults

The maker does not cycle.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check water supply
  • Test valve and heater
  • Check temperatures
  • Inspect sensors

Household ice makers rely on a steady water supply, correct freezer temperature and working controls. A fault in any of those can stop production.
