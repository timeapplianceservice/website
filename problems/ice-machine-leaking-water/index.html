Overview

Why Ice Machine Leaking Water Happens

Ice Machine Leaking Water is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as drain blockage, loose or failed fittings or reservoir overflow. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Water on the floor
  • Puddle under the machine
  • Water around the bin
  • Dripping from fittings
  • Overflow
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Shut off the water supply if needed
  • Check the drain line
  • Look at fittings
  • Note where the water appears

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Drain blockage

Water cannot leave the machine.

Loose or failed fittings

Connections leak.

Reservoir overflow

Float and valve faults cause overflow.

Cracked components

Reservoirs and tubes can crack.

Bin drain problems

Melt water backs up.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Trace the leak
  • Inspect drain and fittings
  • Test valves and float
  • Check reservoir and bin drain
