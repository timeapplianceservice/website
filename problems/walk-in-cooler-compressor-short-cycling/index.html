Overview

Why Walk-In Cooler Compressor Short Cycling Happens

Walk-In Cooler Compressor Short Cycling can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are high head pressure, low charge and electrical issues, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Compressor starts and stops repeatedly
  • Short run times and warm box
  • Loud clicking at the condensing unit
  • Breaker tripping
  • High-pressure shutdowns
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the condenser coil for dirt
  • Ensure the condenser fan is turning
  • Note how long the unit runs before shutting off
  • Do not reset breakers repeatedly

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

High head pressure

Dirty coils or a failed fan shut the unit off on pressure.

Low charge

A refrigerant shortage triggers low pressure cutouts.

Electrical issues

Weak capacitors and contactors create erratic starts.

Control faults

Pressure controls and thermostats can misbehave.

Compressor overheating

Heat and stress trip the overload.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Measure pressures and temperatures
  • Check the condenser and fan
  • Test electrical components
  • Evaluate controls and compressor amps

Indoor and outdoor condensing units fail from heat, dirt and electrical wear. We check each of these and repair the failing component.
