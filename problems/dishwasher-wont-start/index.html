Overview

Why Dishwasher Won't Start Happens

Dishwasher Won't Start is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as door latch failure, control board faults or power issues. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No lights
  • Door will not latch
  • Button not responding
  • Hums but does not start
  • Error code
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the breaker
  • Check the door latch
  • Try a reset
  • Check the child lock

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Door latch failure

The dishwasher will not start.

Control board faults

Controls do not respond.

Power issues

Power does not reach the unit.

Thermal fuse

The unit is dead.

Motor problems

It hums but does not run.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check power
  • Test latch
  • Check board
  • Test fuse and motor

Modern dishwashers rely on sensors, pumps and control boards in addition to heaters and spray arms. We test each part to find why the cycle fails.
