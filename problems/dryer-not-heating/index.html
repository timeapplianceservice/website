Overview

Why Dryer Not Heating Happens

Dryer Not Heating can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are failed heating element, tripped thermal fuse and bad igniter or valve, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Drum tumbles but clothes stay wet
  • No heat
  • Warm but not hot
  • Long cycles
  • Burning smell
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the lint filter and vent
  • Check the breaker (electric dryers have two)
  • Check the gas supply on gas dryers
  • Make sure the vent is not crushed

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Failed heating element

Electric dryers lose heat.

Tripped thermal fuse

Overheating blows the fuse.

Bad igniter or valve

Gas dryers do not light.

Clogged vent

Restricted airflow causes overheating.

Thermostat faults

Heat is not regulated.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check airflow and vent
  • Test element or igniter
  • Test fuse and thermostats
  • Check controls

Gas and electric dryers fail in different ways, but airflow problems are involved in many calls. We check the vent along with the dryer to keep operation safe.
