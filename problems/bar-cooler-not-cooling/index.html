Overview

Why Bar Cooler Not Cooling Happens

When you are dealing with bar cooler not cooling, the cause is rarely the whole back bar cooler. It is usually one failed part, most often dirty condenser, gasket and door wear or fan failure. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Food safety: if product has been above 41°F for an extended period, move perishable items to working refrigeration and follow your local health department guidance.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Bottles warm
  • Cooler running constantly
  • Glass doors fogging
  • Fans not moving
  • Condenser dusty
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Clean the condenser if accessible
  • Check the gasket and door
  • Make sure there is space around the unit
  • Check the thermostat setting

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Dirty condenser

Bar dust and heat reduce cooling.

Gasket and door wear

Constant opening wears seals.

Fan failure

Airflow stops.

Electrical issues

Start parts weaken.

Overloading

Packing the cooler blocks airflow.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Measure interior temperatures
  • Clean and inspect condenser
  • Test fans and electricals
  • Inspect gaskets

Bar coolers get opened constantly and sit in warm, crowded spaces. We check airflow, condensers, gaskets and fans, and repair the part that is causing the problem.
