Overview

Why Air Conditioner Not Turning On Happens

When you are dealing with air conditioner not turning on, the cause is rarely the whole air conditioner. It is usually one failed part, most often tripped breaker or blown fuse, float switch tripped or thermostat or low-voltage fault. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No response from the thermostat
  • Outdoor unit silent
  • Breaker tripping
  • Indoor unit not starting
  • Display blank
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the breaker and disconnect
  • Check thermostat batteries and settings
  • Look for water in the drain pan that may trip a float switch
  • Wait a few minutes for protective delays

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Tripped breaker or blown fuse

Power is cut to the system.

Float switch tripped

A clogged drain shuts the system off.

Thermostat or low-voltage fault

The signal does not reach the equipment.

Failed capacitor or contactor

The outdoor unit cannot start.

Control board problems

The board does not send power to parts.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Verify power and control voltage
  • Test thermostat and wiring
  • Check float switch and drain
  • Test capacitor, contactor and board

Air conditioners fail in familiar ways: weak capacitors, worn contactors, tired fan motors, frozen coils and clogged drains. Our technicians check each of these and explain the findings before any repair begins.
