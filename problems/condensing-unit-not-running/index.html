Overview

Why Condensing Unit Not Running Happens

Condensing Unit Not Running is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as electrical failure, tripped pressure control or compressor failure. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Food safety: if product has been above 41°F for an extended period, move perishable items to working refrigeration and follow your local health department guidance.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No sound from the condensing unit
  • Compressor not starting
  • Fan not turning
  • Breaker tripped
  • Humming without starting
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check disconnect and breaker
  • Look for tripped safety controls
  • Check that the thermostat or controller is calling
  • Do not repeatedly reset breakers

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Electrical failure

Contactors, capacitors and relays fail.

Tripped pressure control

High or low pressure shuts the unit off.

Compressor failure

Overheating or windings can fail.

Power issue

Loss of a phase or voltage drop stops operation.

Control faults

Thermostat or board faults leave the unit idle.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Verify power and voltage
  • Test contactor and capacitors
  • Check pressure controls
  • Measure compressor windings and amps

Indoor and outdoor condensing units fail from heat, dirt and electrical wear. We check each of these and repair the failing component.
