Overview

Why AC Capacitor Failure Happens

When you are dealing with ac capacitor failure, the cause is rarely the whole AC capacitor and contactor. It is usually one failed part, most often heat and age, electrical stress or motor strain. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Humming outdoor unit
  • Fan will not start
  • Compressor hard starts
  • AC clicks but does not run
  • Bulging capacitor
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Turn the AC off
  • Do not touch electrical parts
  • Note whether the fan spins when pushed (do not do this with power on)
  • Call us for testing

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Heat and age

Capacitors weaken over time.

Electrical stress

Voltage swings shorten life.

Motor strain

A failing motor overworks the capacitor.

Poor ventilation

Heat around the unit shortens life.

Manufacturing wear

Some capacitors simply fail early.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Test the capacitor's rating
  • Test motors
  • Inspect contactor and wiring
  • Verify operation after repair

If the outdoor unit hums but the fan does not turn, a weak capacitor is often the reason. We test it, confirm the cause and correct it.
