Overview

Why Commercial Coffee Machine Leaking Happens

Commercial Coffee Machine Leaking can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are worn gaskets and fittings, failed inlet valve and cracked tank or tubing, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Water under the machine
  • Dripping from the spray head
  • Leaking tank
  • Loose fittings
  • Overflow
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Turn off the water supply
  • Check fittings
  • Look at the spray head
  • Note when it leaks

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Worn gaskets and fittings

Seals wear and leak.

Failed inlet valve

The tank overfills.

Cracked tank or tubing

Wear causes cracks.

Clogged spray head

Water backs up.

Overflow faults

Level controls fail.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Trace the leak
  • Inspect fittings and gaskets
  • Test valve
  • Check tank and tubing

Commercial coffee equipment depends on clean water and working heating systems. We repair the parts that fail: elements, valves, sensors, pumps and control boards.
