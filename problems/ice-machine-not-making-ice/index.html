Overview

Why Ice Machine Not Making Ice Happens

When you are dealing with ice machine not making ice, the cause is rarely the whole commercial ice machine. It is usually one failed part, most often water supply problems, scale buildup or sensor or float failures. Testing in the right order is what separates a quick fix from a repeat visit.

Food safety: if product has been above 41°F for an extended period, move perishable items to working refrigeration and follow your local health department guidance.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No ice in the bin
  • Machine running but no ice
  • Error light flashing
  • Water not filling
  • Machine shutting off
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the water supply valve
  • Verify the bin is not full
  • Check the condenser for dirt
  • Note error codes

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Water supply problems

Clogged filters or failed valves stop fill.

Scale buildup

Scale coats the evaporator.

Sensor or float failures

Controls stop production.

Condenser problems

Dirty condensers slow production.

Refrigeration faults

Cooling capacity is lacking.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Check water supply and filter
  • Inspect evaporator and distribution
  • Test sensors and controls
  • Check condenser and refrigeration

When an ice machine slows down or stops, drinks and food service slow down with it. We find whether the cause is water, refrigeration or controls and fix it.
