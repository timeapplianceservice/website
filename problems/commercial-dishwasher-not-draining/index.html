Overview

Why Commercial Dishwasher Not Draining Happens

Commercial Dishwasher Not Draining can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are clogged drain or screen, failed drain valve and pump problems, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Water standing in the tub
  • Slow drain
  • Gurgling sounds
  • Overflow
  • Error code
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Clear the scrap screen and drain
  • Check the drain hose
  • Make sure the machine is level
  • Look for debris

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Clogged drain or screen

Food debris blocks flow.

Failed drain valve

The valve does not open.

Pump problems

The pump is weak or jammed.

Drain line restriction

The building drain slows flow.

Control faults

Drain cycle fails to start.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Inspect drain and screens
  • Test drain valve or pump
  • Check the building drain
  • Inspect controls

High-temp and low-temp commercial dishwashers each have different parts and checks. We test the machine's water temperature, wash action and chemical delivery.
