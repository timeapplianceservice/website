Overview

Why Walk-In Freezer Defrost Problems Happens

Walk-In Freezer Defrost Problems is one of the most common service calls we handle. In most cases it comes down to a small number of causes, such as failed defrost heater, faulty timer or control or termination sensor failure. A proper diagnosis finds the real cause before you pay for a repair.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • Defrost cycle never ends
  • Ice not clearing
  • Heavy frost on coil
  • Frozen drain line
  • Fans icing over
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check for ice on fans and drain
  • Note defrost frequency
  • Avoid manual chipping
  • Record temperatures during defrost

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Failed defrost heater

Heater elements burn out.

Faulty timer or control

Defrost may not start or finish.

Termination sensor failure

Defrost runs too long or not at all.

Drain heater failure

Water refreezes in the line.

Air infiltration

Humidity overwhelms the defrost cycle.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Test heaters and amps
  • Check timer or controller
  • Verify termination sensor
  • Inspect drain heater and line

Frozen product, ice cream and prepared inventory all depend on a freezer that holds steady. We diagnose the cause of warm temperatures instead of just clearing ice and leaving.
