Overview

Why Blower Motor Not Running Happens

Blower Motor Not Running can look alarming, but it is often repairable. The likely culprits are failed capacitor, worn bearings and control module issues, and each has distinct signs. Below you will find what to look for, what you can safely check and what a technician tests.

Symptoms

Symptoms You May Notice

  • No airflow
  • Humming from the air handler
  • Blower runs intermittently
  • Burning smell
  • Furnace or AC shutting off
Try First

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Check the breaker and switch
  • Check the filter and access panel
  • Listen for humming
  • Turn the system off if you smell burning

These checks are meant to be safe for most people. If anything involves gas, electrical panels or refrigerant, stop and call a technician.

Diagnosis

Most Common Causes

Failed capacitor

The motor cannot start.

Worn bearings

The motor seizes.

Control module issues

Variable-speed motors lose signals.

Overheating

Restricted airflow stresses the motor.

Wiring faults

Loose connections interrupt power.

Professional Diagnosis

What a Technician Checks

  • Test power and control signals
  • Test the capacitor
  • Check motor amps and bearings
  • Inspect blower wheel

Blower problems show up as weak airflow, noise, overheating or a system that stops. We confirm the cause and repair it.
