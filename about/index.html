Our Company

Who We Are

Time Appliance Repair serves restaurants, businesses and homeowners across the Charlotte area. We repair commercial refrigeration, ice and kitchen equipment, diagnose and repair existing HVAC systems, and service residential appliances from premium and everyday brands.

Our approach is simple: diagnose first, explain what we found in plain language, quote the repair before work begins and stand behind the work. Our service call is $135. If you approve the repair, the service call is free. If you decide not to repair, the $135 applies. Completed repairs include a 30-day labor warranty and a 90-day parts warranty.

We serve 20 communities across North Carolina and South Carolina, including Charlotte, Ballantyne, Pineville, Matthews, Mint Hill, Stallings, Indian Trail, Weddington, Wesley Chapel, Marvin, Waxhaw, Monroe, Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Mooresville, Concord, Fort Mill, Tega Cay and Indian Land. Our technicians are insured.

Our Process

How We Work

  1. 1

    Request service

    Tell us about the appliance, the symptoms and your address. The first available operator contacts you to confirm the request and the earliest appointment.

  2. 2

    Diagnosis

    We diagnose before we repair, checking power, controls and mechanical parts so the repair targets what actually failed on your equipment.

  3. 3

    Clear quote

    Findings first, price second, repair third. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  4. 4

    Repair

    We carry common parts for popular brands, and explain plainly when a part has to be ordered.

  5. 5

    Test and warranty

    We test the appliance before we leave. Completed repairs include a 30-day labor warranty and a 90-day parts warranty.
