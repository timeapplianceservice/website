What Causes This — And How We Fix It

If your furnace won't start at all, we check the electrical and safety components first — often the fastest and least expensive fix.

What We Check

✓ Tripped breaker or blown fuse
✓ Closed gas valve or tripped safety switch
✓ Failed thermostat or dead batteries
✓ Clogged filter triggering a safety shutoff
✓ Condensate line blockage (high-efficiency furnaces)

What to Have Ready When You Call

  • • Whether the thermostat screen is on
  • • Whether you hear any clicking at the unit
  • • Any error/blink codes on the furnace control board
