Overview

Equipment Repair for Cafes & Bakeries

Cafes and bakeries sell what they display and brew. A warm display case, a stalled ice machine or a brewer that will not heat can ruin a morning rush. We repair the refrigeration, ice, baking and coffee equipment behind the counter.

Small kitchens have little room for backup equipment, so quick diagnosis and honest advice matter. We aim to get you back in service as soon as possible.
