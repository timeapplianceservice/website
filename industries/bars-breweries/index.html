Overview

Equipment Repair for Bars & Breweries

For a bar, cold drinks and plenty of ice are the product. When a back bar cooler warms up or the ice machine stops on a Friday night, revenue stops with it. We repair the refrigeration and ice equipment that keeps the bar running.

Brewery taprooms and kitchens add walk-ins, glycol and food service equipment to the mix. We service the refrigeration and kitchen equipment we are qualified to work on, and we are straightforward about what we can and cannot do.
